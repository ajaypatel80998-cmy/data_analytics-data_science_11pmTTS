# What is Google Business Profile Manager?

-Google Business Profile Manager — formerly known as Google My Business — is a free, easy-to-use tool that lets businesses and organizations manage their online presence across Google's products, including Search and Maps.

-When someone searches for a business name on Google, a business profile card appears showing key details like images, hours, address, and a summary. Business owners can claim this profile and manage it themselves through Google Business Profile Manager — it is the main way to control the information that appears about a business on Google.


---

# Uses of Google Business Profile Manager

* **Manage Business Info** – Update address, phone number, website, and hours
* **Add Products & Services** – Showcase menus, products, or services offered
* **Respond to Reviews** – Reply to customer feedback directly
* **Post Updates** – Share offers, events, and announcements
* **View Insights** – See how customers find and interact with the business
* **Local SEO** – Improve visibility in local search results and the "local pack"
* **Build Trust** – A complete, active profile boosts customer confidence
* **Drive Traffic** – Get calls, direction requests, and website clicks directly from the listing
